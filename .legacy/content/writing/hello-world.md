Welcome to my personal academic website. This space is my digital home for everything I do as a lecturer, researcher, and speaker — and for the things I keep learning along the way.

## What you will find here

- **Research** — my research interests and selected projects in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, and Data Science.
- **Teaching** — the courses I teach at Universitas Islam Indonesia (UII), from text analytics and artificial intelligence to deep learning.
- **Writing** — longer reflections on technology, academic life, and beyond.
- **Speaking** — seminars, workshops, and guest lectures, as opportunities arise.

## About this site

This website is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/), and is fully bilingual: English and Bahasa Indonesia. Both languages are first-class — switch between them any time using the **EN / ID** control in the navigation bar.

Thank you for visiting, and feel free to reach out through the [contact page](/contact).
